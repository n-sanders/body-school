## Connect the levels
Choose the stomach as an example. Its muscle cells are part of muscle tissue. That tissue works with lining, connective, and nervous tissues in the stomach. The stomach then cooperates with other digestive organs.

The levels describe **organization**, not separate boxes. An organ still contains cells, and those cells still need oxygen and nutrients.

## Follow three connections
- A material: oxygen moves from lungs to blood to body cells.
- Another material: nutrients move from the digestive tract into transport routes and on to cells.
- Information: nerves carry signals that can change muscle activity.

## Use the model
Imagine that food is broken down normally but nutrients cannot cross the intestinal lining well. Would normal digestion alone guarantee that cells receive enough nutrients? Explain the missing connection.

**Check your explanation:** It should mention absorption and delivery, not just name the intestine. It should also connect a small structure, the lining, with the needs of the whole body.

The quiz revisits earlier ideas. Use the feedback to repair your explanation before moving on.


**Explain aloud:** Choose one structure from this lesson. Explain how its structure supports its job and how another body part helps it work.
