## The fast message

Touch a hot pan and your hand is gone before you finish the thought "hot." That speed is the **nervous system**.

It has two big parts:

- The **central nervous system** is the brain and the spinal cord.
- The **peripheral nervous system** is the nerves that connect the center to the rest of the body.

**Sensory** signals travel in from the eyes, ears, skin, and other sensors. **Motor** signals travel out to muscles and glands.

## Three regions, not a whole map

You do not need every fold of the brain.

- The **cerebrum** is the large wrinkled part. Thinking, sensing, and voluntary movement depend on it.
- The **cerebellum**, at the back and lower, helps balance and smooth movement.
- The **brainstem** keeps basic jobs going, including breathing rate and heart rate.

The **spinal cord** carries signals up and down and handles some reflexes on its own. Vertebrae guard it. That is why the spine lesson and this lesson share a picture even though one is bone and one is nerve.
