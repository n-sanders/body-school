Your body needs a steady supply of oxygen and a way to remove carbon dioxide. The circulatory and respiratory systems work together to do both jobs. The respiratory system brings oxygen into your body and sends carbon dioxide out. The circulatory system carries those gases, along with other materials, to every cell.

The main parts of the respiratory system are the lungs and the airways that lead to them. When you breathe in, air travels down your windpipe and into the lungs. Inside the lungs are millions of tiny air sacs. Oxygen from the air passes through the thin walls of these sacs and into the blood. At the same time, carbon dioxide moves from the blood into the air sacs so you can breathe it out.

> **Activity idea:** Click on the parts of the respiratory system in the correct order that air travels—from the nose or mouth all the way to the air sacs.

The circulatory system is made of the heart and blood vessels. The heart is a strong muscle that pumps blood. Blood vessels are the tubes that carry blood. Blood vessels have more specific names depending on their job. Arteries carry blood away from the heart, and veins bring it back. Capillaries are the tiniest vessels; they reach almost every cell so oxygen and nutrients can leave the blood and waste can enter it.

Blood picks up oxygen in the lungs and carries it to the heart. The heart then pumps the oxygen-rich blood out through arteries to the rest of the body. After the cells use the oxygen, the blood returns to the heart carrying carbon dioxide. The heart sends that blood to the lungs so the carbon dioxide can be exhaled and fresh oxygen can be collected again.

> **Activity idea:** Sort the pictures into two groups—parts of the respiratory system and parts of the circulatory system. Then match each part to its main job: pump blood, carry oxygen, or exchange gases.

Together these systems keep a continuous loop going: lungs supply oxygen, the heart and vessels deliver it, and the lungs remove carbon dioxide. Without this teamwork, your cells would not get the oxygen they need to stay alive.
