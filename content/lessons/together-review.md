## Your capstone explanation
Choose one scenario:

- A person walks upstairs and then rests.
- A meal is digested and its materials reach working cells.
- A warning sound helps someone stop a movement.

Describe **three systems**, one moving material, and one control signal. Explain what each contributes and how the connections allow the event to happen. Aim for four or five clear sentences, not a long organ list.

## Test the explanation
Now change one link: reduce an exchange surface, interrupt a route, or prevent a responding tissue from receiving its signal. Predict a specific consequence and explain the missing connection.

## Look back at your foundation
You can now connect cells, tissues, organs, and systems; trace supplies and wastes; compare messages with materials; and explain feedback. Scientific names help label those ideas, but the connections are what later courses will build on.

**Self-check:** Did you explain a route? Distinguish observation from inference? Describe a response and its limits? If one part is unclear, return to that lesson and improve just that part before the quiz.


**Explain aloud:** Choose one structure from this lesson. Explain how its structure supports its job and how another body part helps it work.
