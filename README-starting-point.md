# Body School — Starting Point Notes

The course is the nine-lesson sequence in [content/curriculum.yaml](content/curriculum.yaml). Lesson files are in [content/lessons/](content/lessons/).

## Scope

- Middle-school level: concrete examples, limited specialized vocabulary, and a focus on major organs and clear functions.
- The sequence is organization, skeletal and muscular, circulatory and respiratory, digestive, urinary, nervous, reproductive, and immune, then how the systems work together.
- The reproductive lesson stays at the level of its reading: the organs, egg and sperm cells, and how a baby begins and grows.
- Each lesson is a reading, activity ideas, and five concept questions. The activities are placeholders, not interactive tasks. There are no figures yet.

## Suggested next steps

1. Turn the activity ideas into real activities.
2. Source or create diagrams and record credits in `IMAGE_CREDITS.md` and `docs/image-manifest.json`.
