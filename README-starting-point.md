# Body School — Starting Point Notes

This folder contains the unit plans that serve as the genesis documents for the curriculum, modeled after the Unit-*-Plan.txt files in dig-school.

## Scope decisions
- Middle-school level: concrete examples, limited specialized vocabulary, focus on major organs and clear functions.
- Reproductive organs and systems are outside the scope (as requested). Brief notes appear in relevant plans so writers stay consistent.
- No single public API equivalent to Dog CEO exists for appropriately tagged human-organ images. Expect to curate diagrams and illustrations (OpenStax Anatomy & Physiology has many CC-BY figures; Wikimedia Commons; NIH resources) and to create or commission simple clean illustrations where needed, similar to dig-school.
- Servier Medical Art (SMART) have options for clean, accurate, educational anatomy diagrams under CC BY 4.0 license

## Image considerations
- Prefer clear, labeled or unlabeled diagrams suitable for middle-school eyes.
- Avoid graphic clinical photos unless carefully chosen and age-appropriate.
- OpenStax Anatomy & Physiology (college-level but free and well-illustrated) can be a source of simplified figures; many are reusable under CC BY.
- Servier Medical Art (SMART) is a source of individual images
- For a catalog, pin specific reviewed images per organ the way dog-school pins breed photos.

The plans are intentionally written in the same style as dig-school’s Unit plans so the writing and review process can stay consistent.
